# Hall responsibilities

[User Guide](README.md) › Hall responsibilities

Who to contact about the beamline systems that the accelerator and the experimental halls share.
The responsible groups that sign off each system in SRM are on the
[Group Responsibility report](reports.md#group-responsibility); the **Links** tab has the
responsibility documents for the other halls and the beam dumps.
