Checklists and links¶

User Guide › Checklists and links

Checklists¶

A checklist says what a group actually verifies before it signs off the components of a system. There is one for each pairing of a responsible group and a system, so two groups that sign off the same system each have their own. Publishing the checklists means that anyone, from the next group in line to the Crew Chief, can see what a Ready from that group stands for.

Anyone can read the checklists and their history without logging in.

Finding a checklist¶

On the Checklists tab, choose a group. The page lists the systems the group is responsible for:

Column Meaning
System The system the checklist is for.
Checklist Required Whether the group must publish a checklist before it can sign off this system. An admin sets this on Setup › Group Resp.
Published Whether it is published, and by whom and when.
Checklist View, History and, depending on who you are, Create, Edit, Delete and Sign & Publish or Unpublish.

View opens the checklist with its document ID, group, system, author, revision number, date and comment. Printer Friendly opens it as a page of its own for printing. Published checklists are also linked from the Group Information, System Information and Component Information dialogs elsewhere in SRM.

Writing a checklist¶

  1. Log in, choose your group on the Checklists tab, and select Create beside the system (or Edit if there is one already).
  2. Fill in the Author: the person who is responsible for the content, who needn't be the person typing.
  3. Write the checklist in the editor. Bulleted list items are shown with a checkbox, so write each check as a bullet. The Insert template button offers two outlines, Uncredited Control Level 1 and Uncredited Control Level 2, with headings for system validation and for the system's performance specification documents.
  4. When editing, give a Revision Comment saying what changed.
  5. Select Save.

The editor keeps text formatting, headings, lists and links. It doesn't keep images or tables.

Every save is kept as a revision. History lists them, oldest first, with the revision number, date, comment, author and who submitted it; View shows the checklist as it was. An old revision can't be restored, but its text can be copied into a new one.

Publishing¶

A group leader or an admin publishes a checklist with Sign & Publish, which records their name and the date, and can Unpublish or Delete it. Deleting removes the checklist with all of its history.

Not published Published
Create, Edit Any logged-in user A leader of the group, or an admin
Sign & Publish, Unpublish, Delete A leader of the group, or an admin A leader of the group, or an admin

Editing a published checklist leaves it published, with the new revision showing at once.

Required checklists¶

Where Checklist Required is Yes and the checklist isn't published, the group can't sign off that system: the Signoff tab says that a checklist "must be published before signoffs can be made" and lists no components. Admins are exempt. To see which checklists are still missing, use the Group Responsibility report.

Links¶

The Links tab collects references that are useful during a checkout:

  • General: the logbooks, the task lists, the beamline map, the songsheets, the hall and beam dump responsibility documents, and the Accelerator Bypassed-Interlocks Log (on site only).
  • Beamline Sketches: the CEBAF Element Database's sketch of each part of the machine, from the injector to the halls and the LERF.

Admins change the links in the LINKS_GENERAL_CSV and LINKS_SKETCH_CSV settings, on the Setup tab, and the tab shows them at once.
